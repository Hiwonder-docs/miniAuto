# 4. App Control

## 4.1 App Installation

**iOS users:** Search for [Wonderbot]() in the App Store to download the app.  

**Android users:** [Wonderbot]()

## 4.2 App Connection

:::{Note}

* Make sure to enable both Bluetooth and GPS in your phone’s settings before using the app.

* Use the Bluetooth button within the app to pair and connect to the device. Do not use the phone’s system Bluetooth settings to pair.

:::

(1) Turn on the **miniAuto** and launch the **Wonderbot** app. Tap <img src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image2.png"  /> to select **miniAuto** under the “Robot” section.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image3.png"  />

(2) Next, tap the **flashing Bluetooth icon** <img src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image4.png"  /> on the main screen. In the Bluetooth device list, find **"Hiwonder"** and tap to connect.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image5.png"  />

:::{Note}

If **"Hiwonder"** does not appear, tap **"Search Again"** to refresh the list.

:::

(3) Once connected, the **Bluetooth icon** <img src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image4.png"  /> in the top right corner will remain solid. The **battery level** will also appear on the left side of the screen.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image6.png"  />

### 4.3.2 Live Camera Feed

(1) Tap <img src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image10.png"  /> in the upper right corner to open the live camera view, then select **“Connect Hotspot”**.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image21.png"  />

(2) Go to your phone’s **Wi-Fi settings**, find **“HW_ESP32S3CAM”**, and connect to it.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4\section_1\media\image22.png"  />

(3) Return to the app to enter **image transmission mode**, where you can view the **live camera feed**.

<img class="common_img" src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_1/media/image23.png"  />
